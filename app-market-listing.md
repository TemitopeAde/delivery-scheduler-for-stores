Delivery Scheduler

## Teaser

Let customers schedule delivery or pickup.

## Features

- Offer delivery and pickup scheduling
- Configure lead times, cutoffs, hours, holidays, and closures
- Prevent overbooking with slot capacity controls
- Manage scheduled orders from a fulfillment dashboard
- Configure product-specific preparation rules
- Export and update scheduling information in bulk

## Full description

Delivery Scheduler helps online store merchants offer flexible delivery and pickup scheduling. Customers can select an available date and time slot before completing their order, while merchants control availability using lead times, booking windows, cutoff times, working hours, holidays, closures, and capacity limits.

Manage scheduled orders from a centralized dashboard grouped by date and time slot. Review delivery and pickup orders, identify orders that need attention, configure product preparation rules, and export fulfillment information when needed.

The app is designed for stores that need a more organized fulfillment process without replacing checkout or payment processing. Scheduling availability is validated on the server to help prevent invalid selections and overbooking.

The scheduling experience requires placement of the app’s supported site component on an eligible site page. Availability may depend on the selected store configuration and supported extension locations.
